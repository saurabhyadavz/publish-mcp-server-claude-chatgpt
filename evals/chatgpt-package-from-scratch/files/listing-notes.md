# Habitat listing notes

- App name: Habitat
- One line: Log habits in a sentence and see your streaks.
- What it does: you tell ChatGPT what you did ("ran 5 km", "read 20 pages") and Habitat records
  it; ask how a streak is going or what you track. Habits and logs live in your Habitat account.
